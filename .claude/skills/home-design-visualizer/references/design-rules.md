# Design rules of thumb and standard sizes

Rules of thumb, not laws — explain the reason when you apply one, and say when breaking it is fine.

## Proportion
- Sofa width ≈ ½ to ⅔ of the wall it stands against. Under ½ looks lost; over ¾ feels cramped.
- Leave at least 30–45 cm between sofa end and a corner or side table; ~15 cm minimum from a wall corner.
- Art or mirror above a sofa/console: about ⅔ of the furniture's width; bottom edge 20–25 cm above the sofa back; center of art ~145–155 cm from the floor when on an empty wall.
- TV: screen center roughly at seated eye height (~100–110 cm). Viewing distance ≈ 1.2–1.6 × screen diagonal for 4K (a 55" TV ≈ 165–225 cm).
- Coffee table: ≈ ½–⅔ of sofa length, height level with or 5 cm below seat height, 40–50 cm from sofa front.
- Rug in a living room: front legs of all seating on the rug. Common sizes: 160×230, 200×290, 240×340. Rug wider than the sofa by ~15–20 cm each side.
- Curtains: rod 10–20 cm above the window frame (or near ceiling for height), extend 15–25 cm past each side.

## Circulation (walkways)
- Main walkway: 90 cm minimum, 100+ comfortable.
- Between furniture pieces you pass between: 75–90 cm.
- Around a dining table: 90 cm from table edge to wall/furniture for pulling out chairs.
- In front of a wardrobe with hinged doors: door width + ~30 cm. Sliding doors: 70 cm.
- Bed: 60–75 cm clear on the sides people use; 75–90 cm at the foot.

## Color and material
- 60-30-10: dominant (walls/large surfaces) / secondary (sofa, curtains) / accent (pillows, art).
- Tie the new piece to something existing: repeat the floor's wood tone in a leg or frame, or pick a fabric that bridges floor and wall.
- Light, small rooms: lighter, low-contrast palette, leggy furniture that shows floor beneath it.
- Mix at least three textures (e.g. woven fabric, wood, metal or ceramic) to avoid a flat look.
- Grey sofas: warm greys work with oak and beige floors; cool greys with white/grey porcelain floors.

## Lighting
- Three layers: ambient (ceiling), task (reading lamp), accent (wall lights, LED strip, candle-like). Most Israeli living rooms have only the first — suggest adding the other two.
- Warm white 2700–3000K for living/bed rooms.

## Practical (Israeli homes)
- Hot sunny rooms: favor fabrics with UV-resistance; avoid dark velvet near a south/west window.
- Kids/pets: performance fabrics, removable covers, rounded table corners.
- Mamad (safe room) used as a bedroom/office: the steel door and window must stay unobstructed.
